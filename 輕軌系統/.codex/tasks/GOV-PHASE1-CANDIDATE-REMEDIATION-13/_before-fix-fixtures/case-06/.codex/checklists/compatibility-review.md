# Compatibility Review Checklist

- Verify source provenance, deidentification and read-only evidence.
- Verify evidence files and SHA256 before relying on results.
- Check before/after business, relationship and schema hashes.
- Check rerun safety, transaction rollback, forced-failure rollback and backup restore.
- Account for pre-existing and migration-added anomalies separately.
- Preserve `NO-GO` while any formal-clone, P-to-C or shortage/inventory blocker remains.
- Never manufacture evidence or lower a gate.
